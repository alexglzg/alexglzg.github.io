"Constant-Rate Convergence for an Adaptive Sliding Mode Controller Using a Fixed-time Disturbance Observer" was accepted in *International Journal of Robust and Nonlinear Control*. This paper introduces an observer-based reaching law for a constant rate convergence of a sliding mode surface, validated using an underactuated surface vessel. This research was in collaboration with Tecnológico de Monterrey and Université de Technologie de Compiègne.
