I am a Ph.D. researcher at the [MECO Research Team](https://www.mech.kuleuven.be/en/pma/research/meco) at KU Leuven, supervised by [Prof. Jan Swevers](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=X_fnO1YAAAAJ&hl=en). My research revolves around safe motion planning and robust control applied to autonomous systems in water, ground, and air. I focus my work on real-world applications and validations, aiming to develop robots that increase safety and efficiency in dull or dangerous tasks. 

Alongside my graduate studies, between 2021 and 2024, I was a recurrent visiting researcher at MIT’s [CSAIL](https://www.csail.mit.edu/research/distributed-robotics-laboratory) and the [Senseable City Lab](https://senseable.mit.edu/), working with [Prof. Daniela Rus](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=910z20QAAAAJ&hl=en). I hold an M.S. in Engineering (2022) and a B.S. in Mechatronics Engineering (2020) from Tecnologico de Monterrey, Mexico. There, I conducted my undergraduate and master's research within the [Cyber-Physical Systems group](https://tec.mx/en/research/cyber-physical-systems) under the supervision of [Prof. Herman Castañeda](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=aAbtWIkAAAAJ&hl=en). During my undergraduate studies, I also completed a research stay at the [Aerospace Mechatronics Lab](https://aerospacemechatronics.lab.mcgill.ca/) at McGill University with [Prof. Inna Sharf](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Ys2mNPkAAAAJ&hl=en), and led the student group [VantTec](https://www.vanttec.com/) in multiple [RoboNation](https://robonation.org/) competitions.
