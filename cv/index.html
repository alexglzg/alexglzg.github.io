Research Projects

  • 2022 - 2026

    Agile & Reliable Navigation (ARENA)
    Flanders Make SBO project, KU Leuven
    Mobile robot motion planning in highly complex but structured environments.
    • Motion planning framework via a geometric abstraction of the free space.
    • Simulation environments for multiple mobile robots, including unicycle (differential drive), bicycle, and truck-trailer systems.
    • Experiment design for multiple motion planning frameworks in real-world environments.
    • Experimental setup of a “mobile arena” to test autonomous behaviors on real-world robots.
    • Deployment and maintenance (hardware and software) of multiple mobile robots, including commercial differential-drive platforms and truck(-trailer) prototypes.
  • 2021 - 2024

    Roboat
    MIT & AMS Institute
    Autonomous urban vessels.
    • Control system design and deployment for ASVs subject to payloads and disturbances.
    • Motion planning for ASVs in urban environments, deployed in scaled environments.
    • Framework design and algorithm development for a self-reconfiguring system of modular ASVs, deployed on real prototypes of the swarm system.
    • Hardware and software development and maintenance of multiple ASV prototypes.
  • 2017 - 2022

    VantTec
    Student group, Tecnologico de Monterrey
    Research and development of unmanned autonomous vehicles. Head and researcher (2017–2020), then research advisor and mentor (2020–2022).
    • Teleoperation, mechanical design, and manufacturing of multiple USV prototypes.
    • Mathematical modeling and identification of USV systems.
    • Guidance, navigation, and control design, development, and deployment for USVs.
    • Control systems research for UUV systems.
    • Led software development for the simulation and autonomy stacks of USV and UUV systems.
